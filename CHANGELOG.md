## 0.1.0

- First test theme. Surfaces and syntax come from a ten-color palette taken from Guillermo del Toro's *Frankenstein* shots. Syntax colors are lifted tints of those hues. A single electric pale cyan, `#a8e6f2`, is used only for the cursor, matching brackets and find matches.
